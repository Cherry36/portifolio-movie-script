Also worth including

Other formats to add over time

A working writer's portfolio is more than finished scripts. These are the supporting pieces that show range and process — add them as you make them.

01

Loglines

One-sentence pitches for ideas you haven't written yet. Shows you can generate concepts fast.

02

Treatments / Synopses

A one-to-three page prose walkthrough of a story, before it's formatted as a script.

03

Character bios

Backstory, want vs. need, and voice notes for your key characters.

04

Shot lists / storyboards

Even rough sketches show you think visually, not just in dialogue.

05

Table reads / audio-video

A recorded reading of a scene, even on your phone, brings dialogue to life for a reader.

06

Adaptations

A short scene adapted from a novel, news story, or true event — shows you can rework material.

Craft focus

Why dialogue gets its own section

Producers and readers often judge a whole script by a handful of dialogue lines. Keep a running file of your sharpest exchanges — even out of context — and feature the best three or four here on their own, separate from full scripts.
